What I see different

  • 360 and 320 (blocking): on each GitLab connection row the Edit button is clipped by the card: 13 px of its 44 px width is visible at 360 and none at 320, so its label cannot be read or tapped. The four buttons (Edit, Verify, Rotate webhook secret, Remove) are wider than the row. From 390 px up all four fit.
  • 768 and 1280 (cosmetic): on a GitLab row the Project/Group badge drops to a second line and the path is cut off with an ellipsis, because the four buttons take most of the row.
  • Connections page, all widths (intended): one new row, “GitLab – No GitLab projects or groups are connected yet.”, until a GitLab connection exists.
  • Add connection (intended): GitLab appears as a third choice.
  • Webhooks list at 1280 (intended): a GitHub badge now precedes each row’s destination; a GitLab row shows a GitLab badge and no destination text. At 768 and 360 the badge is hidden (by design), so a GitLab delivery looks like a GitHub one in the list there; the detail sheet shows the badge at every width.
  • Delivery detail sheet (intended): a badge beside the heading; a GitLab delivery’s Destination reads “—”.
  • Not changed: the new-pool form, its Targets picker at rest, pool settings of a repository pool and the Runners list are pixel-identical to before at all three widths.
  • Seen, not caused by #141: the Targets dropdown is clipped by its card (only the search box shows when it opens), exactly as in #145’s advisory note.
