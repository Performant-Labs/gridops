Revised after review (finding 8). The cap is the shared 64 characters (schemas.ts and the server), so the issue’s 255 is not drawn. The 64-character error appears at submit in the existing callout, not live under the field: gitlabLabelError checks only non-empty and no comma, and the shared schema owns the cap. They remain a validator defence (gitlabLabelError, Vitest with ["a,b"] and [""]) for payloads that bypass the field. The walkthrough therefore cannot show a live comma error. See open question 2.
