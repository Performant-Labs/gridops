## Summary
BLOCK, on one new finding. The amendment fixes both round-1 blocks:
- Gap 4 handles GitHub's two-scope assumption, and `github.tsx` is now on the allowlist.
- The GitLab pool fields follow the Bitbucket pattern in `schemas.ts` and `runner-pools.functions.ts`.

All seven round-1 warns are also taken up.

The new block is about the pool settings form. The brief and wireframe E5 take the GitLab target options from "the already-loaded `gitlabConnections`". On settings, the only loaded data is the pool detail. Its `gitlabConnections` lists the connections the pool already uses (`pool_gitlab_connections`, a membership join, on master since #138 merged). So a settings form built from it can never offer a second connection. The brief's own walkthrough step ("add a second GitLab connection and save") would then fail. The fix stays inside `gitlab.tsx` and needs no shared-file change. It is a short amendment to the brief.
