What the two sides are. This page is new and no design mock or preview exists for it, so there is nothing to measure it against. The comparison here is before and after the rework: "before" is the round 1 capture (head 45dfed6), "after" is today's capture (head e634843), taken the same way (headless Chromium, the same sample data, full page). It shows the rework changed nothing visible. It does not show that the page matches a design. The real sections (#195, #202, #203) carry their own screenshot checks.

What I see different

  • The small grey build line at the very top of the page changed from "build 465 · 45dfed6" to "build 469 · e634843". That is the build number and commit id, which change with every commit. It is the only change at every size, in light and dark, and in the 403 state.
  • Nothing else moved or changed colour. The sidebar, the page header and window picker (1h, 24h, 7d, Refresh), and all six "Coming in #N" cards are identical to round 1 at 360, 768 and 1280.
  • The rework was an accessibility fix, not a visual one: the sidebar used to tell a screen reader that two items (Machines and Health) were the current page, while only Health looked selected. It now announces only Health. A pixel comparison cannot show that, so it was checked in the browser directly: on /fleet/health exactly one sidebar link carries the "current page" marker, Health, at 360, 768 and 1280 in light and dark.

No other visible differences were detected at any viewport.
