## Summary
BLOCK, with 1 block finding and 5 warns. The direction is right: fix the input to `choose_platform` in core, read ownership from `queued_job_owners`, and leave the `NoDemand` guard alone. But the brief does not say which seam to extend, and the options it names lead into the public GitHub-shaped queue views. Those views also feed the GitHub webhook's scale target, the reconciler's built-in scale-up and eviction, and the built-in provider choice. #148's binding A-B2 keeps them GitHub-only, and their pinned assertions make the brief's own Scope 3 impossible to meet that way. The brief must name an internal seam before T writes tests against it.

**Checked and consistent (no finding):**
- **Layer.** Fixing the input to `choose_platform` in `gridops-core/src/fleet/targets.rs` is the right layer. Placement decisions live in core, and the reconciler only uses the `behind` flag. Special-casing GitLab in the reconciler's `provision`, e.g. `|| is_gitlab_pool && assigned > active`, would be a parallel path, and the brief rightly does not plan one. Leaving the `NoDemand` guard alone is correct.
- **One ownership rule.** `queued_job_owners` with `gitlab_job_candidates` already owns pending GitLab jobs under #148's tag and `run_untagged` rules (A-B4), so the brief's ban on matching labels again is right.
- **Schema.** No migration is needed. Ownership, `runners.target_repository_id` (NULL for GitLab runners) and `workload_intents` already hold everything required.
- **Bitbucket.** Leaving Bitbucket untouched fits the code. Its exclusion from fleet targets is the brief's claim, which the brief gate checked.
- **Coexistence.** No live worktree edits these files. `cool-davinci-1830ec` (#143) is an older branch with a clean tree, and `gridops-134` is older still. #171, #172 and #173 are merged into master (e9e6f52, fe08e3d, a15004b).
